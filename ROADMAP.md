# Roadmap

1. **Run AI extraction and review the outputs.** The operator starts the remaining saved jobs in batches of 100 per click. Sample education, skills, conditional requirements, experience ranges and source quotations. Keep GPT-5.4 mini, medium reasoning and Flex; no separate model evaluation.
2. **Keep collection locations reliable.** Munich uses its verified LinkedIn city identifier. Retain country and city searches; improve explainable filters from reviewed examples. Collection-day targets are unnecessary.
3. **Audit the revised filters.** Sample active and automatically archived jobs against reviewed title preferences, including ML engineering, Cloud/Azure and lead roles. ML scientist and ordinary senior data roles remain eligible; description-specific rejections stay individual archive decisions.
4. **Validate descriptions and historical results.** Review saved German and English postings for text fidelity and listed criteria, including identical roles across cities. Check old parsed fields against saved text, normalize schema differences, and distinguish missing values from unsupported claims. Missing descriptions keep candidates separate; unavailable pages and failed refreshes must preserve usable saved text.
5. **Restore useful Intelligence from extraction.** Add skills, tools, languages, and work-arrangement views with coverage indicators and evidence. Use only reviewed field behavior in conservative filters.

The implementation plan for AI extraction is in [roadmap_ai.md](roadmap_ai.md).

Fit ranking, richer run-history browsing, and generating new AI summaries remain deferred. Preserve existing profiles, scores, parsing records, review data, and run history.

Indeed, automatic scheduling, email reports, AI filtering, application tracking, and the old trend calculations remain outside the current scope.
